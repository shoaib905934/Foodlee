Refunds and cancellations

Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel any order before it is dispatched, by WhatsApp, email or the contact page, quoting your order number. Prepaid orders are refunded in full.
  • Once an order is out for delivery, it can no longer be cancelled.
  • For 30-day plans, deliveries not yet dispatched can be cancelled and refunded for the unused part.

Damaged, leaking, wrong or missing items

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with your order number and a photo of the product and its packaging. After checking, we will send a replacement or refund the value of the affected items, whichever you prefer.

Returns

For food safety and hygiene, we cannot accept returns of opened or used food products, or returns because of a change of mind. If you are unhappy with the taste of a product, tell us anyway: we read every message, and we may offer a goodwill credit.

How refunds are paid

  • UPI payments are refunded to the same UPI account within 5–7 working days of approval.
  • For cash-on-delivery orders, we refund to a UPI ID you share with us.
  • You will get a message when your refund is processed.

Contact

For any cancellation or refund, email support@foodlee.in or WhatsApp .
