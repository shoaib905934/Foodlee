Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Foodlee (“Foodlee”, “we”) collects when you use www.foodlee.in or the Foodlee app, and how we use it. We follow applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address, landmark, pincode, delivery slot, items ordered and amount paid.
  • Location (optional): only if you tap “Use my current location”, to help our rider find you.
  • Payment reference: the UPI transaction ID you share to confirm a payment. We never see or store your UPI PIN, card or bank details.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form, WhatsApp or email.

How we use it

Only to take and deliver your order, confirm payment, contact you about your order, handle refunds and support, and improve our products. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Where it is stored

Order and contact details are stored in our secure Google Workspace account and our WhatsApp Business account. Your cart, saved address and order history are also kept in your own browser or app on your device so you do not have to retype them.

Who we share it with

Only with the people who deliver your order (your name, phone, address and location), and with authorities if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for delivery, accounting and legal requirements, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information at any time by emailing support@foodlee.in. You can clear the information saved on your device by clearing your browser or app data.

Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: email support@foodlee.in or WhatsApp .
