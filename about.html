About Foodlee

Tasty food you can actually trust.

Foodlee is a food brand from Hyderabad. We make everyday food that tastes good and tells you exactly what is inside it.

We started with one simple frustration: most “healthy” food either tastes like medicine or hides sugar, syrups and refined oil behind a nice label. Foodlee exists to fix that, one product at a time.

Our first range is Bee Pure: single-serve shots of raw, unheated honey blended with real ingredients like roasted garlic, ginger, dates, almonds and spices. One squeeze, no sticky spoon, no guesswork.

Our three rules

Nothing heated

Our honey is never heated, so it keeps its natural enzymes and flavour.

Nothing hidden

Every ingredient is on the label and on our website, with the amount per serving.

Nothing fake

No sugar syrup, no artificial colour, no preservatives, no refined oil.

How we make it

  • We buy raw honey directly from beekeepers and test each lot for added sugar, moisture and heating.
  • Dry ingredients are roasted and ground in small batches, then folded into the honey at room temperature.
  • Every batch rests for three days and is checked again before it is packed and dispatched.
  • Each sachet carries a batch code, so every order can be traced back to its honey lot.

What comes next

Bee Pure is the first step. Next on the Foodlee shelf: healthy snacks with no maida or palm oil, fresh-milled atta and millet flours, unpolished dals, spices ground without colour or fillers, and cold-pressed oils that are never refined.
