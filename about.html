About Foodlee

Quality and purity, for everyone.

Pure food shouldn't be a luxury, and it shouldn't come with fine print. Foodlee is a Hyderabad food brand making everyday food that is honest about what's inside, priced for every kitchen.

What we stand for

“Quality and purity aren't a luxury. Everyone deserves them.”

Eat Wiselee.

We started with one simple frustration: most “healthy” food either tastes like medicine or hides sugar, syrups and refined oil behind a nice label. And the genuinely pure stuff is often priced out of reach. Foodlee exists to fix both, one product at a time.

Our first range is Bee Pure: single-serve shots of raw, unheated honey blended with real ingredients like roasted garlic, ginger, dates, almonds and spices. One squeeze, no sticky spoon, no guesswork.

What “Eat Wiselee” means

Eating wisely isn't about diets or rules. It's about knowing what you eat, and choosing food that is good for you and still tastes great. We make that choice simple: read our label, and you know everything.

Our three promises

Pure

No sugar syrup, no artificial colour, no preservatives, no refined oil. Our honey is never heated, so it keeps its natural enzymes and flavour.

Honest

Every ingredient is on the label and on our website, with the amount per serving. Nothing hidden.

For everyone

Small sizes and fair prices, so good food fits every budget, not just a few.

How we make it

  • We buy raw honey directly from beekeepers and test each lot for added sugar, moisture and heating.
  • Dry ingredients are roasted and ground in small batches, then folded into the honey at room temperature.
  • Every batch rests for three days and is checked again before it is packed and dispatched.
  • Each sachet carries a batch code, so every order can be traced back to its honey lot.

What comes next

Bee Pure is the first step. Next on the Foodlee shelf: healthy snacks with no maida or palm oil, fresh-milled atta and millet flours, unpolished dals, spices ground without colour or fillers, and cold-pressed oils that are never refined. Same promise on every pack: pure, honest, for everyone.
