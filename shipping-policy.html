Delivery

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Where we deliver

We currently deliver across Hyderabad and Secunderabad, including Gachibowli, Madhapur, Kondapur, Hitech City, Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Kukatpally and Begumpet. If your area is not listed at checkout, choose “Other” and we will confirm whether we can reach you before dispatch.

Delivery slots

You choose a slot at checkout: tomorrow morning, tomorrow evening or this weekend. We confirm your slot by WhatsApp or phone after you order.

Charges

  • Orders of ₹299 or more: free delivery.
  • Orders below ₹299: ₹40 delivery charge, shown before you pay.

Order confirmation and tracking

Every order gets an order number starting with FL-. You can see its status (Confirmed, Packed, Out for delivery, Delivered) on the Orders screen of our website or app.

Delays and failed deliveries

If we expect a delay, we will tell you before your slot. If nobody is available to receive the order, our rider will call you; we may reschedule once at no charge. Orders that cannot be delivered after two attempts are cancelled and prepaid amounts are refunded as described in our refund policy.

Questions

Contact us: email support@foodlee.in or WhatsApp .
